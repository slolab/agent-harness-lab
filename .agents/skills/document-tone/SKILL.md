Please thoroughly review and refine the document to improve tone, readability, and information prioritization.

You tend to make prose heavier than necessary through rhetorical emphasis, repeated conclusions, unnecessary qualification, parenthetical content, dramatic phrasing, cumbersome asides, and overly assertive language. You also often explain straightforward points at greater length than the reader needs.

You have good attention to detail, but tend to pack secondary nuances, caveats, examples, alternatives, and exploratory observations into the main document, which hurts readability and obscures the important parts. Thoroughly analyze the document and distinguish what is necessary for its purpose and intended audience from material that is useful but secondary. Keep the main document focused and move secondary material into optional [name]_sidenotes.md.

Documents also often arise via an iterative process and become a frankenstein monster of patchwork with references to previous version and outdated or wrong assumptions. This can become very distracting and confusing. Please make sure the document is a well-structured, standalone, final, coherent text.

First, finalize the global structure. Then, fo through the document paragraph by paragraph. Remove unnecessary verbosity, repetition, rhetorical emphasis, editorializing, redundant explanation, and cumbersome asides. Rewrite affected passages into clear, economical prose while preserving all information essential to the document's purpose. Move useful but nonessential material to the sidenotes file so the main document remains focused and easy to read. Keep the writing direct, precise, compact, natural, and easy to follow. Prefer plain English (for human-facing documents, refer to ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English and the EASE guidelines), concrete statements, and continuous sentences over abstraction, rhetorical commentary, or parenthetical constructions.
